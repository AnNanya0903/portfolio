# PDF/AI CAD Matching Stack

PDF catalogue matching now runs on per-page fingerprints generated by Gemini vision (when configured) and matched via text embeddings, with a perceptual-hash fallback for fully offline matching. Autodesk Platform Services has been removed—no Forge/Design Automation credentials are required.

## Deployment Topology
- MagicWand backend dashboard (Django) continues to consume the CAD service over HTTPS.
- Supporting microservices live under `microservices/`; the CAD service is now PDF-only and serves inline previews directly.

## Components
- `microservices/cad-service/` – FastAPI service for catalogue listing, PDF uploads, matching, and previews.
- `microservices/cad-service/pdf_matcher.py` – CLI/helpers to build and query PDF fingerprint indexes.
- `docs/` – Deployment notes for Cloud Run/App Engine (unchanged for PDF flow).

## Prerequisites
- Python deps: PyMuPDF (`fitz`), Pillow, `imagehash`; optional `google-generativeai` when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set for Gemini vision fingerprints + text embeddings.
- No APS/Forge credentials or buckets are needed for matching.

## Quick Start
```bash
cd microservices/cad-service
pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn app:app --reload --port 8300
```

## Workflow
1. Upload catalogue PDFs via `POST /catalogue/upload` or drop files under `CAD_STORAGE_DIR`.
2. Each page is fingerprinted (Gemini vision when configured, otherwise perceptual hash) and matched via cosine similarity.
3. Call `POST /pdf/match` or `POST /cad/match` to get per-page similarity scores; responses include the top matches with verdicts.
4. The dashboard renders PDFs directly from `/cad/preview/{id}` or downloads via `/cad/download/{id}`—no APS viewer needed.

## Endpoints
- `POST /catalogue/upload`
- `GET /cad/drawings` and `GET /cad/drawings/page`
- `POST /cad/match` (PDF per-page matches)
- `POST /pdf/match`, `POST /pdf/catalogue/index`
- `GET /cad/preview/{drawing_id}`, `GET /cad/download/{drawing_id}`
- `GET /uploads/{upload_id}/status`

## Notes
- APS/Forge-specific routes and tokens have been removed; set `GEMINI_API_KEY` to improve match quality.
- Storage paths (`CAD_STORAGE_DIR`, `CAD_UPLOAD_DIR`) remain configurable; catalogue PDFs are listed from these locations.

## KYC by shareable link (`kyc` app)
Staff send a customer a private link; the customer fills the KYC form and uploads documents without signing in; Accounts or Top Management approve or reject. The rules live in `kyc/link_requests.py`.

**Flow**
1. On a customer's page, **Send KYC Link** (Sales, Marketing, Accounts, Top Management) picks a recipient email. Known details are prefilled; the link (`/kyc/form/<token>/`) works for `KYC_LINK_EXPIRY_HOURS` (72). Only a SHA-256 hash of the token is stored.
2. The customer asks for a 6-digit code at that email (10 minutes, 5 tries, at most 5 codes an hour), then fills one page that follows AVIGIRI's **Customer Registration Form** (`forms/Avigiri_Customer_Registration_Form.pdf`) section by section: (1) company details: nature of company and business, year, CIN, website; (2) the company, billing and delivery addresses, each with city, district, state and PIN, and "Same as" ticks that copy an address; (3) GSTIN, PAN, MSME (Udyam, category) and certifications; (4) contact details: the Purchase contact is mandatory, and the ➕ adds Managing Director, Design / Technical, Accounts, Quality and Stores / Logistics; (5) transportation, delivery type, preferred transporter (default ARC), products, expected annual business, special requirements; (6) bank details; (7) documents, where only the GST certificate and PAN card are mandatory (Udyam, cancelled cheque, incorporation / partnership deed and ISO / IATF are optional; PDF/JPG/PNG, 5 MB, type checked from the file content); (8) the declaration and consent. A `*` on the paper form is mandatory online too. Payment terms are set by staff and shown read-only. "Save draft" keeps what is filled in, so the same link works until it expires. Submitting locks the form and emails the customer's assigned departments.
   **The PDF is stored, not only rendered.** On every submit, and again on approval, the Customer Registration Form is rendered (`kyc/link_pdf.py`, fpdf2) and saved in private storage as a `generated_form` document named `Customer-Registration-<customer code>-<yyyymmdd-hhmm>.pdf`. Older versions are kept; the newest is marked `is_latest`. "Registration form (PDF)" on the request page and the customer page, and the download on the customer's link (after the OTP), serve the stored file and only render live if none is stored. The customer signs and stamps it and uploads it back as the signed copy. PAN and the bank account number print as the last four characters only; anything not given prints as an empty box to fill by hand.
3. **Fill KYC manually** (customer page; Sales, Marketing, Accounts, Top Management) starts a request that is the staff member's own: no link or email is sent and no OTP is needed; `source` is staff and `filled_by` is recorded. It opens the same form inside the portal. Staff can attach a scanned, signed paper form, or email the customer a link only to confirm what was entered (`confirmed_by_customer_at`). **Fill on behalf of customer** does the same for a request that was already sent: the customer's link stops working. The request shows "Filled by … on …" and the staff member who filled it cannot approve it.
4. **Approve** (Accounts or Top Management) fills the customer's *empty* fields (GSTIN, PAN, address, city, state, PIN, website, transport, delivery type, MSME, Udyam), adds each contact person in their department unless their email is already there, sets the **payment terms** and **credit limit** on the customer, marks the customer verified, and prints the form again with the office-use panel filled in (date of approval, payment terms, credit limit, verified by (Accounts), approved by). The credit limit is set on the request page by Accounts or Top Management; it is internal and is printed only after approval. **Reject** needs a reason and the items to reopen; the customer gets a new link where only those can be changed.
5. **Resend** ends the old link and issues a new one with a fresh expiry; **Revoke** ends it. The dashboard at `/kyc/requests/` filters by status (Sent, Opened, In progress, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Expired, Revoked), searches, and shows each link's time left.
6. Every hour App Engine cron calls `/kyc/api/cron/kyc-links/` (in `cron.yaml`; `python manage.py kyc_link_jobs` does the same): links past their time become Expired, and a reminder email goes once per link 48 hours after sending.

**Try it locally**: `python manage.py migrate kyc` (migrations `0015` to `0017`), then run the server, open a customer under KYC and use **Fill KYC manually** or **Send KYC Link**; with `KYC_LINK_DRY_RUN` on, the link and the OTP are shown on screen instead of being emailed. `python manage.py test kyc` runs the tests.

**Security**: PAN and bank account numbers are Fernet-encrypted and shown as `XXXXXX1234`; only Accounts and Top Management can reveal the full number, and each reveal is logged. Documents are stored under random names in private storage and served only through the signed-in download view. Public endpoints are rate limited (Django cache; configure a shared `CACHES` backend so the limits hold across App Engine instances; the OTP limits are also enforced in the database). Every action is in `kyc_request_audit_log` with user or customer, time and IP. No Aadhaar number is collected.

**Configuration** (see `.env.example`): `KYC_LINK_DRY_RUN` (on by default: nothing is emailed; staff see the link and the public page shows the OTP), `KYC_LINK_EXPIRY_HOURS`, `KYC_LINK_REMINDER_HOURS`, `KYC_LINK_BASE_URL`, `KYC_LINK_SENDER` (Gmail user for OTPs, reminders and notices; staff actions send from the staff member's Gmail), `KYC_FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` (required when `DEBUG` is off), `KYC_PRIVATE_BUCKET` / `KYC_PRIVATE_DIR`, `KYC_UPLOAD_MAX_BYTES`. Run `python manage.py migrate kyc` (migration `0015_kyc_link_requests`) and deploy `cron.yaml`.
