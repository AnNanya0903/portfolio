# PDF/AI CAD Matching Stack

PDF catalogue matching now runs on per-page fingerprints generated by Gemini vision (when configured) and matched via text embeddings, with a perceptual-hash fallback for fully offline matching. Autodesk Platform Services has been removed—no Forge/Design Automation credentials are required.

## Deployment Topology
- MagicWand backend dashboard (Django) continues to consume the CAD service over HTTPS.
- Supporting microservices live under `microservices/`; the CAD service is now PDF-only and serves inline previews directly.

## Components
- `microservices/cad-service/` – FastAPI service for catalogue listing, PDF uploads, matching, and previews.
- `microservices/cad-service/pdf_matcher.py` – CLI/helpers to build and query PDF fingerprint indexes.
- `docs/` – Deployment notes for Cloud Run/App Engine (unchanged for PDF flow).

## Prerequisites
- Python deps: PyMuPDF (`fitz`), Pillow, `imagehash`; optional `google-generativeai` when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set for Gemini vision fingerprints + text embeddings.
- No APS/Forge credentials or buckets are needed for matching.

## Quick Start
```bash
cd microservices/cad-service
pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn app:app --reload --port 8300
```

## Workflow
1. Upload catalogue PDFs via `POST /catalogue/upload` or drop files under `CAD_STORAGE_DIR`.
2. Each page is fingerprinted (Gemini vision when configured, otherwise perceptual hash) and matched via cosine similarity.
3. Call `POST /pdf/match` or `POST /cad/match` to get per-page similarity scores; responses include the top matches with verdicts.
4. The dashboard renders PDFs directly from `/cad/preview/{id}` or downloads via `/cad/download/{id}`—no APS viewer needed.

## Endpoints
- `POST /catalogue/upload`
- `GET /cad/drawings` and `GET /cad/drawings/page`
- `POST /cad/match` (PDF per-page matches)
- `POST /pdf/match`, `POST /pdf/catalogue/index`
- `GET /cad/preview/{drawing_id}`, `GET /cad/download/{drawing_id}`
- `GET /uploads/{upload_id}/status`

## Notes
- APS/Forge-specific routes and tokens have been removed; set `GEMINI_API_KEY` to improve match quality.
- Storage paths (`CAD_STORAGE_DIR`, `CAD_UPLOAD_DIR`) remain configurable; catalogue PDFs are listed from these locations.

## KYC by shareable link (`kyc` app)
Staff send a customer a private link; the customer fills the KYC form and uploads documents without signing in; Accounts or Top Management approve or reject. The rules live in `kyc/link_requests.py`.

**Flow**
1. On a customer's page, **Send KYC Link** (Sales, Marketing, Accounts, Top Management) picks a recipient email. Known details are prefilled; the link (`/kyc/form/<token>/`) works for `KYC_LINK_EXPIRY_HOURS` (72). Only a SHA-256 hash of the token is stored.
2. The customer asks for a 6-digit code at that email (10 minutes, 5 tries, at most 5 codes an hour), then fills one page that follows AVIGIRI's **Customer Registration Form** (`forms/Avigiri_Customer_Registration_Form.pdf`) section by section: (1) company details: nature of company and business, year, CIN, website; (2) the company, billing and delivery addresses, each with city, district, state and PIN, and "Same as" ticks that copy an address; (3) GSTIN, PAN, MSME (Udyam, category) and certifications; (4) contact details: the Purchase contact is mandatory, and the ➕ adds Managing Director, Design / Technical, Accounts, Quality and Stores / Logistics; (5) transportation, delivery type, preferred transporter (default ARC), products, expected annual business, special requirements; (6) bank details; (7) documents, where only the GST certificate and PAN card are mandatory (Udyam, cancelled cheque, incorporation / partnership deed and ISO / IATF are optional; PDF/JPG/PNG, 5 MB, type checked from the file content); (8) the declaration and consent. A `*` on the paper form is mandatory online too. Payment terms are set by staff and shown read-only. "Save draft" keeps what is filled in, so the same link works until it expires. Submitting locks the form and emails the customer's assigned departments.
   **The PDF is stored, not only rendered.** On every submit, and again on approval, the Customer Registration Form is rendered (`kyc/link_pdf.py`, fpdf2) and saved in private storage as a `generated_form` document named `Customer-Registration-<customer code>-<yyyymmdd-hhmm>.pdf`. Older versions are kept; the newest is marked `is_latest`. "Registration form (PDF)" on the request page and the customer page, and the download on the customer's link (after the OTP), serve the stored file and only render live if none is stored. The customer signs and stamps it and uploads it back as the signed copy. PAN and the bank account number print as the last four characters only; anything not given prints as an empty box to fill by hand.
3. **Fill KYC manually** (customer page; Sales, Marketing, Accounts, Top Management) starts a request that is the staff member's own: no link or email is sent and no OTP is needed; `source` is staff and `filled_by` is recorded. It opens the same form inside the portal. Staff can attach a scanned, signed paper form, or email the customer a link only to confirm what was entered (`confirmed_by_customer_at`). **Fill on behalf of customer** does the same for a request that was already sent: the customer's link stops working. The request shows "Filled by … on …" and the staff member who filled it cannot approve it.
4. **Approve** (Accounts or Top Management) fills the customer's *empty* fields (GSTIN, PAN, address, city, state, PIN, website, transport, delivery type, MSME, Udyam), adds each contact person in their department unless their email is already there, sets the **payment terms** and **credit limit** on the customer, marks the customer verified, and prints the form again with the office-use panel filled in (date of approval, payment terms, credit limit, verified by (Accounts), approved by). The credit limit is set on the request page by Accounts or Top Management; it is internal and is printed only after approval. **Reject** needs a reason and the items to reopen; the customer gets a new link where only those can be changed.
5. **Resend** ends the old link and issues a new one with a fresh expiry; **Revoke** ends it. The dashboard at `/kyc/requests/` filters by status (Sent, Opened, In progress, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Expired, Revoked), searches, and shows each link's time left.
6. Every hour App Engine cron calls `/kyc/api/cron/kyc-links/` (in `cron.yaml`; `python manage.py kyc_link_jobs` does the same): links past their time become Expired, and a reminder email goes once per link 48 hours after sending.

**Try it locally**: `python manage.py migrate kyc` (migrations `0015` to `0017`), then run the server, open a customer under KYC and use **Fill KYC manually** or **Send KYC Link**; with `KYC_LINK_DRY_RUN` on, the link and the OTP are shown on screen instead of being emailed. `python manage.py test kyc` runs the tests.

**Security**: PAN and bank account numbers are Fernet-encrypted and shown as `XXXXXX1234`; only Accounts and Top Management can reveal the full number, and each reveal is logged. Documents are stored under random names in private storage and served only through the signed-in download view. Public endpoints are rate limited (Django cache; configure a shared `CACHES` backend so the limits hold across App Engine instances; the OTP limits are also enforced in the database). Every action is in `kyc_request_audit_log` with user or customer, time and IP. No Aadhaar number is collected.

**Configuration** (see `.env.example`): `KYC_LINK_DRY_RUN` (on by default: nothing is emailed; staff see the link and the public page shows the OTP), `KYC_LINK_EXPIRY_HOURS`, `KYC_LINK_REMINDER_HOURS`, `KYC_LINK_BASE_URL`, `KYC_LINK_SENDER` (Gmail user for OTPs, reminders and notices; staff actions send from the staff member's Gmail), `KYC_FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` (required when `DEBUG` is off), `KYC_PRIVATE_BUCKET` / `KYC_PRIVATE_DIR`, `KYC_UPLOAD_MAX_BYTES`. Run `python manage.py migrate kyc` (migration `0015_kyc_link_requests`) and deploy `cron.yaml`.

## Quotations: plan → approve → the CRO emails it in the customer's own thread (`quotations` app)
Every quotation belongs to an enquiry. The customer's addresses and email thread are kept on the enquiry
(`EnquiryContact`, since the enquiry service itself has no email fields). KYC is not part of this flow.

**Flow** (`quotations/workflow.py`, `quotations/sending.py`)
1. **Quotation team** (`quotation_costing`) uploads the PDF (PDF only, at most `QUOTATION_MAX_PDF_MB`, checked on the server) and the Excel working, then picks **Save as draft** or **Submit for approval**. A new version of a quotation that was already approved always goes back to *Pending approval*.
2. **Top Management** (or `quotation_approval`) opens the PDF inline on the *Pending approval* tab. **Approve** freezes an immutable copy, `quotations/approved/{number}_v{n}.pdf`, and stores its SHA-256 with who approved it and when. **Reject** needs a reason; the quotation goes back to the team (status *Rejected, needs revision*) and the uploader gets a push notification. Anyone else gets 403.
3. **Link enquiry email** (Enquiry Board, or the enquiry page): staff pick the customer's original email from the shared mailbox; its Message-ID, References, subject and Gmail threadId are saved on the enquiry. Enquiries from phone, WhatsApp or a visit can stay unlinked.
4. On approval the status is **Approved, OK to send**; the CRO / Customer Service get a push notification ("QT-xxxxx (ENQ-xxxx, customer) vN approved – ready to send"; by email too with `QUOTATION_READY_NOTIFY_EMAIL`). They are the users given `quotation_send` and the members of the KYC departments in `QUOTATION_READY_NOTIFY_DEPARTMENTS` (default `Sales`). Links to every older version are revoked: their page only says "This quotation has been replaced. Please use the latest link sent to you."
5. **CRO** (`quotation_send`) works from the **Approved, OK to send** tab or the banner on the enquiry page. **Send to customer** first asks for the enquiry when the quotation has none. It always uses the latest approved version; while the latest version is pending or needs revision, nothing can be sent ("Waiting for management approval – do not send an older version."). To is the enquiry's customer email 1, and CC holds its other customer emails plus the enquiry's CCs (never the mailbox itself). The CRO may edit the recipients and the message, but not the version or the PDF. The email carries the approved PDF (`{number}.pdf`) and a new share link tied to that version. The link's password is **never** in that email: it follows in a separate reply in the same thread. If that second email fails, **Send the password again** gives the link a new password and emails it. A linked enquiry email gets a reply in its thread: threadId, In-Reply-To = the latest Message-ID, References = the chain plus that ID, and the subject `Re: <original subject>`, which cannot be edited because Gmail keeps a reply in the thread only when the subject matches. Without a known thread it is a new email, `Re: <enquiry subject>`, with "(Revised)" from version 2 on; its thread and Message-ID are saved on the enquiry for later emails. On success the quotation is *Sent to customer* (vN), the tracker records the sender, recipients, time and Gmail ids, and the follow-up schedule starts again from this email. On failure the link is removed, the quotation stays *Approved, OK to send*, and the error is in the timeline; the CRO can retry. Manual sending works even when automatic emails are switched off. **Resend** sends the same version in the same thread.
6. The CRO records **Customer accepted** (optional PO / reference and note) or **Customer declined** (reason required). Accepting opens **Create Proforma Invoice**, a hook only for now: `Quotation.proforma_unlocked` and the `quotations.workflow.quotation_accepted` signal.

**Quotation ↔ enquiry** (`quotations/enquiry_link.py`): the enquiries live in the enquiry service (their own database), so `Quotation.enquiry_id` holds a checked ENQ number rather than a database key. One enquiry can have many quotations; a quotation has one enquiry. The upload form's searchable **Enquiry number** picker (ENQ, customer, subject) accepts only enquiries that exist, and the Enquiry Board's "Quotation" button preselects it. The customer's own reference (e.g. `AG-QR-ALSTOM-235`) goes in **Customer reference** (`enquiry_reference`). Migration `quotations/0004` moved any such text out of `enquiry_id`. **Change enquiry** (CRO, administrators) is logged in the tracker with who, when and old → new. The board and the enquiry page list each linked QT with its current version and status; the enquiry page also shows the ready-to-send banner and the version history (approved by / on, sent on, revision reason).
**Revisions**: a revision is a new version of the same quotation on the same enquiry; a different item from the same customer is a new QT on the same enquiry; a separate new requirement email is a new enquiry. Enquiries with a linked quotation cannot be deleted. An administrator can **Merge duplicate…** from the enquiry page instead: the duplicate's quotations, tracker, email thread and addresses move to the enquiry kept, then the duplicate is deleted in the enquiry service. **Generate Link** on an enquiry with quotations shares the latest approved version of the QT the CRO chooses, and never falls back to a PDF attached to the enquiry.
**Older quotations**: `python manage.py suggest_quotation_enquiries` (safe to run again) suggests an enquiry for each unlinked quotation. A match needs the same customer (name or email domain) and the same subject after removing Re:/Fw:/Fwd:, AG-QR/AG-RQ/AG-QF codes, dates and punctuation (exact, or one contains the other); among several matches, the one received closest to the quotation's date wins. Nothing is linked until an administrator confirms each suggestion, or picks the right enquiry, on `/quotations/enquiry-suggestions/`.

**Timeline**: every step is a `QuotationEvent` (submitted, approved, rejected, sent, resent, send failed, accepted, declined, email linked), with recipients, Message-ID and errors in `details`. It shows on the quotation and on the enquiry page for every department; internal pricing comments are shown only to costing and management.

**Mailbox setup (Google Workspace, `QUOTATION_MAIL_PROVIDER=GMAIL_API`)**: the portal already uses Google sign-in with Gmail scopes. Sign in to the portal once as `customerservice@avigiri.com`; the portal stores that account's OAuth token on the server and sends and reads the mailbox with it. CROs never need the mailbox password. Gmail keeps every sent email in that mailbox's Sent folder. If the token expires, sending fails with a clear message until someone signs in as the mailbox again. Give CROs the **Quotations: email to customer (CRO)** module, and note that unrestricted users have every module.
For later providers: SMTP needs a Google *App Password* for the mailbox and must BCC it to keep a copy; Microsoft Graph needs an Entra app registration with `Mail.Send` and `Mail.Read` application permissions limited to the mailbox. Both are reserved in `quotations/mailer.py` and currently refuse to send.

**Deliverability**: make sure SPF and DKIM (and ideally DMARC) are configured for `avigiri.com` in the Google Workspace admin console and DNS, so quotation emails are not marked as spam.

**Configuration** (see `.env.example`): `QUOTATION_MAIL_PROVIDER`, `QUOTATION_MAILBOX_ADDRESS`, `QUOTATION_MAILBOX_NAME`, `QUOTATION_VALIDITY_DAYS`, `QUOTATION_MAX_PDF_MB`, `QUOTATION_READY_NOTIFY_DEPARTMENTS`, `QUOTATION_READY_NOTIFY_EMAIL`. Run `python manage.py migrate quotations enquiries` (migrations up to `quotations/0004`, `enquiries/0023`), then `python manage.py suggest_quotation_enquiries`. Tests: `python manage.py test quotations`.
